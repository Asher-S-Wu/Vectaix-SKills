### 02. 金融投资分析

- 所属页面分类：已安装、金融。
- 安装状态：已安装。
- 来源：[打开 金融投资分析 的详情页](https://www.kimi.com/plugins?pluginEnterMethod=plugin_button&pluginId=19f4f5e7-d1f2-825b-8000-000064691980)。

**用途说明：**进行个股、行业、宏观和量化研究，并分析投资组合、风险及估值。

**页面简介（原文）**

投研与组合工作流

**介绍（原文）**

二级市场研究与投资组合工作流插件，覆盖个股研究、盈利与估值、主题与因子筛选、资金流向跟踪、回测、策略复现、宏观研究、组合风险监控及研究报告输出等场景，覆盖 A 股、港股与美股。面向买方与卖方研究员、基金经理及量化研究人员。插件会将每个研究任务结构化地拆解执行，并可访问 Wind、iFinD、Gildata、S&P Global、Yahoo Finance、SEC、IMF、World Bank、天眼查等数据源。输出会标注数据来源，且仅限研究分析，不执行交易。

#### 技能（11 条，原文）

**company-research**（中文释义：公司研究）

Single-company research, fundamentals through earnings lifecycle: tearsheet (default entry), deep dive, initiation, business model, competitive landscape, SEC filings, normalization, earnings preview/review/forecast, calls, assumptions, model update.

**datasource-router**（中文释义：数据源选择与引用）

Route data requests to bundled providers: iFinD, Wind, Gildata, S&P, SEC, Yahoo, Tianyancha, World Bank, IMF, IGO/FRED, Xinhua CNFIC, Caixin, finance_research (sell-side research), Binance. Provider routing and citation only; no analysis.

**finance-dashboard**（中文释义：金融市场看板）

One-click deploy the preset market-daily dashboard (金融看板/市场看板/金融终端) in Kimi Work desktop Canvas: preset widgets, scheduled refresh, morning brief. Triggers: market/finance dashboard, financial terminal. Desktop-only; installs presets, no recoding.

**industry-research**（中文释义：行业研究）

Industry maps, chain economics, cycle staging vs analogs, sector rotation, TAM/SAM/SOM, AI stack. Sub-skills: sector-industry-map, industry-chain-analysis, industry-cycle, sector-rotation-radar, market-sizing, ai-industry-analysis.

**macro-strategy**（中文释义：宏观与投资策略）

Top-down macro and strategy: China/global growth, inflation, rates, credit, policy, cross-asset allocation (SAA/TAA), HK/overseas strategy, Fed, southbound, AH premium. Sub-skills: macro-research, asset-allocation, hk-overseas-strategy.

**market-microstructure**（中文释义：市场交易、资金与情绪分析）

A-share themes and tape, positioning and flow evidence, ETF products: theme rating, leader screen, daily/limit-up review, momentum risk, northbound/dragon-tiger, 13F, short interest/insiders, options sentiment, ETF flows, ETF/index diligence.

**portfolio-management**（中文释义：投资组合管理）

Portfolio management and standing watch: construction, position sizing, risk summary, drawdown/liquidity stress, attribution (Brinson), idea generation, catalyst calendar, thesis tracking with kill criteria, technicals. Analysis only; no orders.

**quant-research**（中文释义：量化研究）

Quant research: universe building, factor screens and IC review, event studies, quick backtests with bias checks, robustness, academic-paper replication, self-evolving qlib loops, transaction-cost simulation. Evidence only; no orders.

**question-router**（中文释义：识别研究问题并选择工作流）

Classify an ambiguous or mixed public-markets request, assemble the ContextEnvelope (market, asset, workflow, depth), and select the single accountable lead main skill. Never picks sub-skills, providers, renderers, or formats.

**report-production**（中文释义：报告与材料制作）

Turn reviewed analysis into deliverables: output-format intake, report assembly, institutional PDF, PPTX decks, deliverable QC, interactive design polish. No analysis or data sourcing.

**valuation**（中文释义：估值）

Valuation: method reconciliation, DCF (WACC, scenarios, sensitivity), trading comps, convertible bonds (parity, terms, bond floor, delta). Sub-skills: valuation-analysis, dcf-model, trading-comps, convertible-bond-analysis.

#### 官方示例（原文）

近期有哪些盈利预期改善、估值合理且资金持续流入的 A 股公司？

### 04. 企业财务会计

- 所属页面分类：已安装、金融。

- 来源：[打开 企业财务会计 的详情页](https://www.kimi.com/plugins?pluginEnterMethod=plugin_button&pluginId=19f4f5e7-4222-837c-8000-0000eeb77517)。

**用途说明：**协助企业关账、对账、编制报表、预测现金流、准备审计底稿及处理税务和基金会计。

**页面简介（原文）**

企业财务与会计工作流

**介绍（原文）**

企业财务与会计工作流插件，覆盖关账、对账、凭证编制、财务报表、现金流预测、管理报表、审计底稿、中国税务及基金会计等场景。面向企业财务、FP&A、财务控制、审计及基金运营团队。插件会将每个工作流结构化地拆解执行，并在需要时从 Wind、iFinD、Gildata、S&P Global、Yahoo Finance、SEC、IMF、World Bank、天眼查等来源获取参考数据。输出会标注数据来源，并在最终定稿前预留人工复核环节。

#### 技能（9 条，原文）

**audit-controls**（中文释义：审计支持与内部控制）

Audit and controls support: audit-ready workpapers with PBC tracking, SOX 404 testing (documentation, sampling, deficiency classification), approval-workflow evidence checks with exception registers. No audit opinion.

**china-tax-vat**（中文释义：中国税务与增值税）

China tax support packages: VAT (small-scale/general), CIT quarterly prepayment and annual settlement, IIT/social insurance, Golden Tax invoice reconciliation, R&D super-deduction review. Never submits filings.

**close-management**（中文释义：月末与年末关账管理）

Period-end close management: close checklists with dependencies and approval gates, trial-balance loading and QC, review-ready manual journal entries (accruals, depreciation, amortization, revenue). Preparation only; no posting.

**datasource-router**（中文释义：数据源选择与引用）

Route data requests to bundled providers: iFinD, Wind, Gildata, S&P, SEC, Yahoo, Tianyancha, World Bank, IMF, IGO/FRED, finance_research (sell-side research). Provider routing and citation only; no analysis.

**fund-accounting**（中文释义：基金会计）

Fund accounting: LP capital-account tie-out to the fund NAV pack (recompute from contributions, distributions, P&L, fees), and distribution waterfall / carried-interest computation per fund terms.

**reconciliation**（中文释义：账务核对）

Reconciliations: bank-to-GL cash matching, GL-to-subledger roll-forwards, intercompany balances with elimination entries - break classification, aging, source lineage, review gates.

**report-production**（中文释义：报告与材料制作）

Accounting deliverables: one-time output-format intake for reports/packs/dashboards/memos, plus automatic visual polish after interactive HTML is selected. No accounting work or data sourcing.

**reporting-fpa**（中文释义：财务报告与规划分析）

Reporting and FP&A: financial statements from TB (CAS/US GAAP/IFRS toggle), statement tie-out with adjustment bridge, CFO dashboards and management packs, variance commentary with driver attribution, cash-flow forecasts.

**working-capital**（中文释义：营运资金管理）

Working capital: AP aging (vendor exposure, duplicate-payment risk, approval readiness) and AR aging/collections (customer exposure, disputes, unapplied cash, allowance inputs), with GL tie-out.

#### 官方示例（原文）
